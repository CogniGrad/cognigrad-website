3. Children’s Data – Special Protections

DPDP Act Section 9: Users under 18 receive heightened protection.
  • Age 13-17: Parental consent required at signup. Parent email verified.
  • No behavioral tracking: We disable advertising cookies for <18 users.
  • No targeted ads: We don’t profile children for marketing.
  • Parental rights: Parents can request access/deletion via Access My Data.
  • Safety recordings: All sessions with minors recorded for POCSO Act compliance. Parents may request access.

You have these rights. Exercise them free of charge:

  • Right to Access s.11: Get a copy of your data → Request Here. Response: 30 days.
  • Right to Correction s.12: Fix inaccurate data. Email privacy@cognigrad.com
  • Right to Erasure s.12: Delete your account/data → Delete Here. Response: 30 days. Note: We may retain some data if legally required (e.g., POCSO recordings, tax records).
  • Right to Grievance Redressal s.13: Contact Grievance Officer. Response: 24hr acknowledgment, 15 day resolution.
  • Right to Nominate s.14: Nominate person to exercise rights if you’re deceased/incapacitated. Email us.
