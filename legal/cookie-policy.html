1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit CogniGrad. They help us provide core functionality, remember preferences, analyze usage, and comply with DPDP Act 2023.

Legal Basis: Per DPDP Act Section 6, we obtain your explicit consent before using non-essential cookies. Essential cookies run on "Legitimate Use" s.7(c).

2. Types of Cookies We Use

Category Purpose Examples Consent Required Duration
Essential Login, security, load balancing. Site won’t work without these. session_id, csrf_token, auth_token No – Legitimate Use Session / 7 days
Functional Remember language, theme, tutor filters lang_pref, theme_dark Yes – Consent 1 year
Analytics Understand usage, improve features. IP anonymized. _ga, _gid (Google Analytics) Yes – Consent 2 years
Marketing Measure ad performance. Disabled for users <18 _fbp, _gcl_au Yes – Consent 90 days
⚠️ Children <18: Per DPDP Act Section 9, we automatically disable Analytics + Marketing cookies for users under 18. Only Essential + Functional cookies run.

5. How to Delete Cookies

You can delete cookies via your browser settings:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy → Clear browsing data
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy → Cookies → Clear
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data

Note: Deleting essential cookies will log you out of CogniGrad.

6. Your Rights – DPDP Act 2023

  • Access Cookie Data s.11: Request list of cookies we set → Access My Data
  • Erasure s.12: Delete all non-essential cookies via browser + we delete server logs in 30 days → Delete My Data
