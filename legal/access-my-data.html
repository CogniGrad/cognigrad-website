  • Processing activities: Identities of all data processors we share with

5. What We CANNOT Provide

Per DPDP Act Section 11(2), we may refuse or redact data if:

  • Trade secrets: Our AI model weights, algorithms, business logic
  • Third-party data: Other users’ data in your chats/recordings (redacted)
  • Legal privilege: Communications with lawyers
  • Ongoing investigation: Data related to fraud/POCSO investigation
  • Technical logs: Raw server logs (we provide summary instead)

If we refuse, we’ll explain why in writing within 30 days.
