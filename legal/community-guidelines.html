1. Purpose

CogniGrad is a global learning community for users 13+. These guidelines ensure all members — students, parents, tutors, contributors — interact respectfully, safely, and lawfully. Violations may result in account action + legal reporting per Indian law.

2. Respectful Conduct & Zero Tolerance

  • Treat all members with courtesy and professionalism.
  • Zero tolerance for: harassment, hate speech, bullying, threats, doxxing, stalking, or discrimination based on age, gender, religion, caste, disability, sexual orientation, or nationality.
  • No sexual content or advances of any kind. Unwelcome flirting, sexual jokes, comments on appearance, or requests for personal photos = immediate termination + police report under POCSO Act if <18 involved.
  • Respect cultural, linguistic, and personal differences.

3. Academic Integrity & Anti-Cheating

  • Do not use CogniGrad to cheat on exams, assignments, or certifications.
  • Plagiarism, uploading copyrighted materials, or sharing exam questions is prohibited.
  • Tutors must provide guidance, not complete assignments. Writing essays, solving take-home exams, or impersonating students = termination + ₹50,000 penalty per Tutor Agreement.
  • AI Use: You may use AI to learn, but submitting AI work as your own without understanding violates academic integrity.

4. Child Safety – POCSO Act 2012 Compliance

⚠️ Criminal Law: Violations involving minors under 18 may result in 3-7 years imprisonment under POCSO Act. No bail.
  • Age 13-17: Parental consent required. Parents may join/monitor sessions.
  • No contact outside platform: Tutors/students must not exchange WhatsApp, Instagram, phone numbers, or personal emails. All communication via CogniGrad only.
  • No meeting in person: Online only. Requests to meet offline must be reported.
  • Mandatory reporting: If you suspect child abuse/sexual offence, report to grievance@cognigrad.com + Childline 1098 within 24 hours. Failure to report is a criminal offence under POCSO Section 21.
  • All sessions with minors are recorded for safety. Recordings stored 180 days minimum.

5. Prohibited Content & Behavior

  • No illegal content: Pornography, violence, drugs, self-harm instructions, terrorism.
  • No inappropriate content: Obscene language, sexual images, graphic violence.
