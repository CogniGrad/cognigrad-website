1. Purpose & Legal Framework

CogniGrad respects intellectual property rights. This policy complies with:

  • Copyright Act, 1957 (India)
  • IT Act, 2000 Section 79 – Intermediary Guidelines & Digital Media Ethics Code, 2021
  • Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA) – For international copyright holders

CogniGrad is an "intermediary" under IT Act 2000. We do not pre-screen user content but respond expeditiously to valid takedown notices. We expect users, tutors, and contributors to upload only original or properly licensed materials.

2. What Is Copyright Infringement

Unauthorized use of: textbooks, PDFs, videos, images, course content, software, exam papers, or any work protected by copyright.

Examples prohibited on CogniGrad:

  • Uploading NCERT/CBSE/ICSE textbook PDFs
  • Sharing paid Udemy/Coursera/BYJU’S course videos
  • Distributing copyrighted exam papers without permission
  • Using copyrighted music/images in lessons without license

3. Reporting Copyright Infringement – Takedown Notice

If you believe content on CogniGrad infringes your copyright, submit a Takedown Notice to our Grievance Officer.

⚠️ Legal Requirement: Your notice MUST include all 7 items below per IT Rules 2021 Rule 3(1)(b). Incomplete notices will be rejected.

Required Information:

  1. Identification of copyrighted work: Title, author, registration number if any, or URL to original.
  2. Identification of infringing material: Exact URL on CogniGrad OR description + screenshot. We cannot act on “all videos” – must be specific.
  3. Your contact info: Full legal name, address, phone, email. No pseudonyms.
  4. Good faith statement: “I have a good faith belief that use of the material in the manner complained of is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.”
  5. Accuracy statement: “I swear, under penalty of perjury, that the information in this notice is accurate and I am the copyright owner or authorized to act on behalf of the owner.”
  6. Electronic/physical signature: Typed name + date counts as electronic signature per IT Act 2000.
  7. Declaration of ownership: Attach proof – copyright certificate, assignment deed, or link to your original work predating upload.

5. Counter Notice – If Your Content Was Removed in Error

If you believe takedown was mistaken, submit a Counter Notice with:

  1. Identification: URL of removed content + date it appeared before removal
  2. Perjury statement: “I swear, under penalty of perjury, that I have a good faith belief the material was removed due to mistake or misidentification.”
  3. Your details: Name, address, phone, email
  4. Jurisdiction consent: “I consent to jurisdiction of Courts in Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh, India for any action related to this counter notice.”
  5. Signature: Electronic/physical
  6. Proof of right: Explain why you have right to use – e.g., “I am author”, “Fair use for education s.52”, “Licensed from publisher”

📧 Send to: copyright@cognigrad.com
Subject: "Counter Notice – [Content Title]"

Process: We forward your counter notice to complainant within 48hrs. If they don’t file court case in 10 business days, we restore content in 10-14 business days per IT Rules.

6. Repeat Infringer Policy – Three Strikes

CogniGrad terminates accounts of repeat infringers per IT Act 2000:

  • Strike 1: Warning + content removed + mandatory copyright training
  • Strike 2: 30-day suspension + content removed
  • Strike 3: Permanent termination + IP ban. No refund.
  • Egregious cases: Single instance of mass piracy = immediate termination

We maintain a register of terminated users for 180 days per IT Rules.

7. Tutor & User Responsibilities – You Are Liable

  1. Upload only what you own or license: Tutors must ensure lesson materials, PPTs, videos, worksheets are original or licensed.
  2. No copyrighted textbooks: Don’t upload NCERT, RD Sharma, HC Verma PDFs. Link to publisher site instead.
  3. Fair Use s.52: Limited quotes for teaching allowed. Full chapters = infringement.
  4. Indemnity: Per Tutor Agreement, you indemnify CogniGrad for copyright claims from your uploads. We will recover legal costs from you.
  5. Prohibited: Recording other tutors’ classes, screen-sharing paid courses, sharing exam paper leaks.

8. Disclaimer & Safe Harbor – IT Act Section 79

CogniGrad is an intermediary and not liable for user-generated content provided we:

  1. Don’t initiate transmission, select receiver, or modify content
  2. Follow due diligence: Publish this policy + appoint Grievance Officer + act on takedowns
  3. Remove content within 36hrs of valid notice

We do not monitor all uploads. If you see infringement, report it. We rely on you.

9. False Claims – Penalty

⚠️ Perjury Warning: Submitting false DMCA/Copyright notice = perjury under IPC Section 191. You may be liable for damages to user whose content was wrongly removed + legal costs. Don’t abuse this system.
