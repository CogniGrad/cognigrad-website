1. Your Rights – POSH Act 2013

✅ ZERO TOLERANCE: CogniGrad has zero tolerance for sexual harassment. Your complaint will be handled confidentially by our Internal Complaints Committee (ICC).

You have the right to:

  • File complaint within 3 months of incident (extendable to 6 months with valid reason)
  • Confidentiality: Your identity, respondent identity, and proceedings are confidential. Breach = penalty per POSH Act s.16
  • No retaliation: You cannot be punished for filing complaint in good faith
  • Interim relief: Transfer, leave, or other relief during inquiry per s.12
  • Appeal: Appeal to court/tribunal within 90 days if unsatisfied per s.18

2. What is Sexual Harassment?

Per POSH Act Section 2(n), sexual harassment includes:

  • Physical contact and advances
  • Demand/request for sexual favors
  • Sexually colored remarks: Jokes, comments on appearance
  • Showing pornography
  • Any unwelcome physical, verbal, or non-verbal conduct of sexual nature
  • Quid pro quo: Implied/explicit promise of preferential treatment or threat of detriment
  • Hostile work environment: Creating intimidating/offensive environment

Applies to: Students, tutors, employees, parents, visitors. Online + offline.
