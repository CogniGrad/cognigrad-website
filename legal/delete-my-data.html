1. What Happens When You Delete

Per DPDP Act 2023 Section 12, you have the right to request erasure of your personal data. Upon verification:

  • Account deleted: Profile, login, preferences removed
  • Content deleted: Homework uploads, chat messages, forum posts anonymized or deleted
  • Subscriptions cancelled: No future billing. No refund for current period per Refund Policy
  • Access revoked: You cannot login or recover data after deletion
⚠️ What We May Retain (Legal Obligation):
  • Transaction records: 7 years for tax audit per Income Tax Act
  • POCSO recordings: If you were <18 or interacted with minors, recordings retained 180 days minimum per POCSO Act, then deleted unless legal case pending
  • Legal disputes: Data retained till case closure if you have active complaint/legal case
  • Anonymized data: Aggregated analytics with no personal identifiers may be retained

2. Deletion Timeline – 30 Days

24 Hours
We acknowledge your request via email per IT Rules 2021
7 Days

30 Days
Data erased from active systems. Backups purged in 90 days per DPDP Act.

Note: If request is complex or you have legal hold, we may extend by 30 days with notice per DPDP Act s.12(3).
